About Us

A Foundation Rooted in Service and Justice

The Barr. A.A. Maigoro Foundation is a non-profit organisation founded to uplift vulnerable people and strengthen communities through education, access to justice and humanitarian support.

Guided by integrity and compassion, we work with local leaders, volunteers and partners to deliver programmes that create lasting change.

Our Mission

To empower the less privileged through education, legal support and sustainable community programmes.

Our Vision

A just society where every person has the opportunity to thrive.

Meet the Founder

Barr. Aliyu Anas (A.A. Maigoro)

DL, LL.B, LL.M, DDP, MSL, MIGL, SLP
Senior Assistant (Legal) to the Honourable Minister of Defence
“The law is not just to argue. It is to protect.”

Barr. Aliyu is a disciplined advocate and strategist. His legal foundation was formed at Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria, where he earned a Diploma and an LL.B in Civil Law, both with distinction. He was called to the Nigerian Bar after training at the Nigerian Law School, Kano Campus.

Leadership defines his career. He served as Senior Advocate at ABU Zaria and the Nigerian Law School Kano, and as President of the Nasarawa State Students Association. He is also Principal Partner & Lead Counsel at Haam Legal Consult, practising in property law, security & military law and civil disputes.
